# Scoring Rubric

This five-point rubric is a supporting signal inside the five-dimension review (`JD 匹配`, `ATS`, `HR`, `面试准备度`, and `可信度`); it is not a substitute for evidence-based review.

Do not assign a zero or a full score by default when a required fact or JD is missing. Mark the relevant dimension as `暂无法评分` and state what evidence is needed.

Score each category from 1 to 5.

| Category | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Role relevance | Mostly unrelated | Partially aligned | Strongly aligned |
| Evidence quality | Claims without proof | Some examples | Specific, credible evidence |
| Keyword match | Few JD keywords | Several relevant keywords | Strong JD keyword coverage |
| Impact | Vague tasks | Some outcomes | Clear outcomes or measurable impact |
| Clarity | Hard to scan | Understandable | Concise and recruiter-friendly |

## Interpretation

- 22-25: strong fit; polish and apply.
- 17-21: reasonable fit; strengthen evidence and keywords.
- 12-16: possible stretch; focus on transferable evidence.
- Below 12: high mismatch; consider a different role or major repositioning.
