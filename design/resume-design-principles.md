# Resume Design Principles

Easy-Job-Tutor should generate resumes that are visually polished but still practical for real job applications.

## Core Design Philosophy

A good resume should be:

- Easy to scan in 10 seconds.
- Clear enough for recruiters.
- Structured enough for ATS systems.
- Specific enough to show evidence.
- Minimal enough to avoid distraction.

## Recommended Style

Prefer modern minimal design.

Use:

- Clean typography.
- A fixed white resume page canvas for preview and export.
- Clear section dividers with subtle accent rules.
- Strong name header inside a restrained profile block.
- Balanced whitespace.
- Consistent bullet spacing.
- Compact contact chips.
- Timeline-like entry grouping for education, experience, projects, and awards.
- A light skills panel when skills are grouped by category.
- Limited accent styling.

Avoid:

- Heavy colors.
- Photos by default.
- Skill bars.
- Infographics.
- Icons.
- Multiple columns unless explicitly requested.
- Complex tables.
- Decorative backgrounds.
- Over-designed templates.

## PDF Delivery Layer

The PDF builder is only the final presentation layer. It should follow the polished HTML-to-PDF feel of VibeResume-style delivery while keeping Easy-Job-Tutor's original JD analysis and resume optimization rules unchanged.

Design expectations:

- The browser preview and exported PDF should use the same screen layout.
- The page should look like a designed resume sheet, not a plain word-processor document.
- Export should remove browser preview chrome, shadows, and background while preserving the internal layout.
- The output must remain text-based, selectable, and ATS-readable.
- If content is too long, reduce density carefully or allow a longer/two-page resume based on candidate level rather than hiding or clipping content.

## Style Options

### Classic Professional

Best for finance, consulting, law, government, education, corporate, administration, accounting, and audit roles.

Characteristics:

- Conservative layout.
- Black and white.
- Traditional section order.
- Minimal styling.

### Modern Minimal

Best for technology, data, AI, product, software engineering, startups, business analyst, and internet roles.

Characteristics:

- Clean spacing.
- Strong headings.
- Light accent line.
- Simple one-column layout.

### Creative Clean

Best for marketing, media, content, branding, design-adjacent, and creator roles.

Characteristics:

- Slightly more personality.
- Optional subtle accent color.
- Still ATS-conscious.
- No excessive decoration.
